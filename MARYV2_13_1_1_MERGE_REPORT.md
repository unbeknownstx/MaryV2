## Skills and agents

The project keeps three meanings separate:

1. coding/development agents such as Codex follow `AGENTS.md` and edit source only;
2. Mary's `agency` / `autonomy` systems represent Mary's own goals, intentions, curiosity and bounded initiative;
3. future task-specialist agents will be temporary workers assembled under Mary's task orchestrator from skills, tools, models and compute nodes. They will not own Mary's identity, memory, relationship or developed self.

See `SKILLS_AGENTS_13_1_1.md` for the next implementation boundary.
