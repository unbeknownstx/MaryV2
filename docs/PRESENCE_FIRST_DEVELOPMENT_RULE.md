# Presence-First Development Rule

MaryV2 development is now evaluated against one experiential north star:

> Does the change make it more convincing that **Mary is actually there** while preserving one coherent Mary Core?

## Before adding a subsystem

1. Name the user-visible problem.
2. Identify Mary's existing canonical owner.
3. Search public implementations and reusable models/assets.
4. Prefer improving the owner over adding another owner.
5. Keep learned/derived systems advisory unless explicitly promoted through an existing governed path.
6. Connect the change through cognition/behavior/performance/surface as appropriate.
7. Add observability without leaking private content.
8. Test focused behavior, broader regression, convergence, and release verification.
9. Distinguish deterministic verification from live experiential validation.

## Priority questions

- Does Mary notice the right things?
- Can she choose silence?
- Does she preserve conversational floor and recover after interruption?
- Does she carry unfinished/relevant context naturally?
- Does she behave differently by relationship and social setting without becoming inconsistent?
- Do voice, expression, gaze and motion realize the same underlying performance intent?
- Can multiple devices contribute capabilities without becoming multiple Marys?
- Can a stronger/smaller model be swapped or blended without redefining Mary?
- Can every important decision be inspected after the fact without storing hidden chain-of-thought?

Projects, dashboards, virtual worlds, streaming, games and production tools remain valuable, but they are environments/capabilities for Mary.  They do not outrank Mary herself.
