## Authority hierarchy

Canonical identity, relationship, memory, personality/developed self, values, agency, permissions, and creator authority remain owned by their existing Mary subsystems. Reservoir/vector indexes, provider text, perception events, task specialists, response feedback, and compute nodes are derived/context/evidence layers only.

## Production dialogue

Production Local Mind V2 is a deterministic front porch for bounded low-risk responses. It retrieves candidate records from a rebuildable reservoir, confirms authority-bearing candidates against canonical owners, projects a typed realization plan, composes locally, independently audits the output, and fails closed to model-backed cognition when anything is incomplete or unsafe.

ResponseRisk refines routing. Precision-sensitive unknowns force the thinking/task route. Open-conversation classification may refine generic character conversation but does not erase a stronger personal-conversation or factual/task classification from TurnPolicy.

## Realtime / perception / distributed compute

AttentionBus unifies bounded event priority. RealtimeInteractionCoordinator tracks listening/transcribing/thinking/responding/speaking/interrupted state and anti-echo. PerceptionDirector separates objective observation from Mary's interpretation. NodeRegistry describes where capabilities can run without making any node a state owner.

## Retrieval and future ML

Lexical/FTS retrieval remains the safe no-model baseline. The vector index is a derived SQLite cache with content hashes; embeddings retrieve candidates but do not establish truth. Explicit response feedback creates a private evaluation/training corpus without becoming memory. Future PyTorch/LoRA/VLM work plugs into these boundaries rather than replacing them.
