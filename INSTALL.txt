Design
------
Natural creator self-statements are checked by a small deterministic gate.
Accepted candidates are handed to the existing RelationshipManager, which
remains the one authoritative creator model.

Accepted categories are intentionally narrow:
- communication preferences
- preferences/dislikes
- interests
- goals
- values

Questions, hypotheticals/uncertainty, and past-only statements are ignored.
Repeated already-known facts do not create duplicate episodic memories or
advance relationship curiosity again.
